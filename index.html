About Us

A wise choice of accommodation Book us for your next trip to Ladakh

We are a small, family-owned hotel in the heart of Keylong, Himachal Pradesh. We take pride in our personalized service and our commitment to making your stay comfortable and enjoyable. Our staff is attentive and knowledgeable, and will go out of their way to make sure your needs are met.

Our location is perfect for exploring all that Keylong has to offer. We are within walking distance of restaurants, shops, and other tourist attractions.

Tariff Notes for Travelers

  • EP – Tariff for room only. No meals included.
  • CP – Tariff includes room rent and breakfast.
  • MAP – Tariff includes room rent with breakfast and either lunch or dinner.
  • AP – Tariff includes room rent with all meals.
  • GST will be charged extra on above tariffs.
  • Children below 5 yrs get complimentary stay.
